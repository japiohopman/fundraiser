# Project Story

## Purpose

This project is an independent information site about the fundraising actions that appeared after the ParkNest fire in Amsterdam's Flevopark in August 2026.

The site exists to make the fundraising landscape easier to understand. It should answer a simple question before a visitor donates:

> **What exactly am I supporting with this donation?**

The site is not an official ParkNest website and must not present itself as representing ParkNest, its organisers, or the fundraising platforms.

## What happened

In the night of 20 to 21 August 2026, ParkNest in the Flevopark was largely destroyed by fire. AT5 reported that the fire started in a difficult-to-reach void beneath the roof and that the building was subsequently allowed to burn out in a controlled way because entering it was too dangerous.

ParkNest's own 22 August statement says the roof collapsed, the walls were badly damaged and almost everything that had been built up there was lost. It also states that the organisation intends to continue its activities and is looking at temporary solutions and a future for ParkNest.

## Why this site exists

There are multiple fundraising actions associated with the aftermath of the fire. They do not necessarily have the same beneficiary, purpose, or use of funds.

The site will therefore separate:

1. **Collective ParkNest fundraising** — support for continuing ParkNest activities and rebuilding its future.
2. **Personal loss/recovery fundraising** — campaigns concerning belongings or collections lost by individual people.
3. **Other campaigns** — added only when their purpose and beneficiary can be documented.

The site will link directly to the original campaign pages so visitors can inspect the source before donating.

## Editorial position

This project should be factual rather than accusatory.

We will not describe a fundraising setup as dishonest, misleading, hidden, fraudulent, or unfair unless a reliable source establishes that claim. In particular, we will not state that a campaign was or was not shared on ParkNest's social media without preserving and documenting evidence for that statement.

When a fact cannot yet be verified, the site data should say **Needs verification** rather than silently turning an assumption into published copy.

## Core message

A useful working line for the site is:

> **One fire. Multiple fundraising actions. A clear view of what each one is for.**

This wording is intentionally neutral: it explains the information problem without telling visitors which campaign they should support.

## Identity and disclosure

The eventual website should clearly state:

> This is an independent information page about fundraising actions connected to the ParkNest fire. It is not the official ParkNest website and does not manage the listed crowdfunding campaigns.

Every campaign card should link to its original source and show when the information on the card was last verified.
