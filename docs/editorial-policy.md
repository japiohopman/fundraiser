# Editorial Policy

## Purpose

The site is an independent transparency and information resource. Its job is to help visitors understand fundraising actions connected to the ParkNest fire without deciding for them which action to support.

## Rules

### 1. Separate fact, source, and interpretation

Every substantive statement should be traceable to a source. Campaign-specific facts should come from the campaign page where possible. Background facts about the fire should prefer ParkNest's own publication and reliable independent reporting.

### 2. Do not infer money flows

A visitor must be able to tell whether a campaign is collective ParkNest support or a personal loss/recovery campaign. Do not state or imply that donations cross from one campaign to another unless a source explicitly establishes that arrangement.

### 3. Avoid accusations

Words such as *dishonest*, *fraudulent*, *misleading*, *hidden*, or *scam* require strong documentary support and should not be used as editorial shortcuts. The site can document confusing or incomplete information without assigning motive.

### 4. Show verification dates

Dynamic values such as amount raised, target progress, campaign status, and social-media visibility must carry a `last verified` date. Never present an old value as live.

### 5. Preserve uncertainty

When a detail has not been independently verified, label it **Needs verification** in project data and keep it out of definitive public copy until checked.

### 6. Link to originals

Every listed campaign must link directly to its original crowdfunding page. Where useful, the site should also link to ParkNest's own statement and independent reporting about the fire.

### 7. Independent-site disclosure

The website must clearly identify itself as independent and must not imply that it is operated by ParkNest or WhyDonate.

## Source hierarchy

1. Original campaign page / platform page
2. ParkNest's own public statements
3. Reliable independent reporting
4. Public social-media posts and archived copies
5. User-provided information or screenshots — useful for leads, but marked as unverified until independently captured

## Update discipline

When a campaign changes, update the source register first. Then update the site's data/content from that register. The website should not become a separate undocumented source of truth.
